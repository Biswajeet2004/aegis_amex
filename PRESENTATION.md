# Aegis — Pitch Deck

## 1. The new systemic risk
One capable agent is manageable. Thousands acting at machine speed can turn a small permission mistake into a material loss before a human can intervene.

## 2. The safety boundary
Aegis is the mandatory control plane between every agent and every financial API. No agent action bypasses authorization, budget reservation, and audit.

## 3. Granular permissions
Policies combine workload identity, action, resource, amount, counterparty, geography, time, risk score, and human approval. Denials return explicit, reviewable reason codes.

## 4. Money-safe concurrency
Hierarchical budgets are reserved atomically before execution and settled afterward. Dynamic caps tighten automatically as risk rises, while velocity limits stop burst behavior.

## 5. Stop one. Stop everything.
Per-agent revocation invalidates one identity. Fleet stop increments a global execution epoch, invalidating all outstanding tokens across every gateway.

## 6. Evidence, not screenshots
Each decision is hash-chained with the policy version, inputs, outcome, latency, and operator context. Evidence is retained in WORM storage and exported to SIEM.

## 7. Built for operators
The control room puts fleet posture, spend, high-risk agents, policy state, and emergency controls in the first viewport. Dangerous changes require two-person approval.

## 8. Validation plan
Golden policy tests, property-based boundary tests, adversarial identity tests, 10k-way spend races, region-failure drills, and end-to-end audit replay.

## 9. Target performance
p95 decisions <10 ms, p99 fleet-stop propagation <2 s, zero concurrent overspend, and 100% policy-version attribution.

## 10. Why Aegis
It lets banks scale autonomous agents without scaling blind trust. The outcome is safe autonomy: fast enough for machines, accountable enough for banking.
